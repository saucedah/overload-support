Overload

Support

Overload is a strength-training log for iPhone and iPad. It keeps everything on your device.

Contact

Questions, a bug, or a request about your data? Write to info@jpshgroup.com.

Please include your iOS version, the app version (Settings › General › iPhone Storage › Overload), and what you expected to happen. Please do not send your workout file unless asked: it is your private data.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my training data stored?

Only on your iPhone or iPad, in the app's own storage. Overload has no accounts and no servers, and the app makes no network connections. The developer cannot see your data. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I back up my data?

Overload's data is part of your device backup. If your iPhone or iPad backs up to iCloud, or to a Mac or PC through Finder, Overload's data is in that backup like any other app's. Check Settings › your name › iCloud › Saves to iCloud (or iCloud Backup) to make sure device backup is on.

Version 1.0 does not have an in-app "export my data" button. Exporting a backup file is planned for a later version.

How do I restore a backup?

There are two ways:

  1. Restore your device backup. Restoring an iPhone or iPad from an iCloud or Finder backup brings Overload and its data back with it.
  2. Import an Overload backup file. In Overload, open your profile, choose Data › Import backup…, and pick the file. The app checks the file and shows a preview first. Nothing changes until you confirm. Overload keeps a safety copy of your current log inside the app (the last 5) before it replaces anything.

Importing replaces your current log with the contents of the file. Only import files you trust.

How do I move to a new phone?
  1. On the new device, use Apple's Quick Start or Restore from iCloud Backup (or Finder) during setup. Overload and its data come across with your other apps.
  2. If you set the new device up as new, install Overload from the App Store link you were given, then import a backup file as described above.

Keep the old device until you have opened Overload on the new one and checked that your history is there. Apple's support site has current step-by-step guides for backing up and transferring an iPhone.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the Overload app from your device (touch and hold the icon › Remove App › Delete App). This removes the app and all of its data from that device. Overload keeps no data anywhere else, so there is nothing for the developer to delete.

Copies in your own device backups (iCloud or Finder) are managed by you: in Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Backups, you can delete the backup or turn off Overload's data in it.

Version 1.0 has no "erase all data" button inside the app; deleting the app is the way to erase everything.

Does Overload need an account, or internet access?

No. There is no sign-in, and the app works fully offline.

Why does Overload ask to send notifications?

Only for the rest timer, so you get a nudge when your rest ends while the app is in the background. These are local notifications created on your device. You can turn them off at any time in Settings › Notifications › Overload, and the app keeps working.

Does Overload give medical advice?

No. It is a logging and training-progression tool. Talk to a qualified professional about pain, injury, or health conditions before training.

My data looks wrong or the app won't open my history.

Close the app fully and reopen it, then use Try again if it is offered. If your history is still missing, restore from your device backup or an Overload backup file (see above), then contact us at info@jpshgroup.com.
