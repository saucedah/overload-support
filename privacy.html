Overload

Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026. Applies to the Overload app for iPhone and iPad, version 1.0.

Data Not Collected

In short: Overload does not collect any data. Your training log stays on your device. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

1. What we collect

Nothing. The developer does not collect, receive, or have access to any information from the app. This is the "Data Not Collected" privacy label on the App Store page.

  • No account, sign-in, name, or email address is asked for or stored.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage statistics.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or health-record access (the app does not use Apple Health).
  • No network connections. The app does not send data to the developer or to anyone else, and it works offline.

2. What is stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following on your device only, in its own storage:

WhatWhy
Your training log: workouts, exercises, sets, weights, reps, reps in reserve, notes, readiness, body weight, schedule, and your settings and exercise swapsIt is the app's purpose. It is stored in a local SQLite database file inside the app.
Safety copies: up to 5 earlier versions of your log, made automatically just before you restore a backupSo you can recover if you restore the wrong file.
A few small preferences and state flags, kept with Apple's standard app-preferences storage (UserDefaults): which one-time hints you have seen, a running rest-timer, your last day-move (for undo), and whether the notification question was askedTo remember how the app was left.

This data is protected by iOS data protection (available after you first unlock the device after a restart). None of it is transmitted by the app.

If your device is backed up to iCloud or to a computer, this data may be included in your device backup, like other apps' data. That backup is yours and is governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy, not by the developer.

3. Notifications

If you allow it, Overload shows a local notification when your rest timer ends. It is created and delivered on your device. It involves no push service and no server, and you can turn it off in iOS Settings.

4. Files you import

If you import a backup file, you choose it with the iOS file picker. The app reads it on your device, checks it, and shows a preview. If you confirm, its contents replace your log. The app does not keep a separate copy of the file and does not send it anywhere.

5. Sharing and third parties

We do not share data, because we do not have any. Overload includes one third-party component, the open-source GRDB database library (MIT licence), which runs only on your device, performs no network access, and collects nothing. There are no analytics, advertising, or other third-party SDKs.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Retention: your data stays on your device until you remove it. The developer holds no copy, so there is nothing held or retained on our side.
  • Deletion: delete the Overload app from your device and all of its data on that device is removed. Because nothing is collected, there is no account or server record to delete and no consent to revoke.
  • Backups: copies inside your own iCloud or computer backups are under your control and can be deleted through your Apple account or Finder.
  • Notifications: consent can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings › Notifications › Overload.

Step-by-step instructions are on the Support page.

7. Health and safety

Overload is a logging tool and does not provide medical advice. Information you enter, such as notes or body weight, is your own and stays on your device as described above.

8. Children

Overload is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it collects no data from any user. It has no accounts, no chat, no advertising, and no third-party analytics.

9. Your rights

Because the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct, export, or delete on our side. You have direct control of the data on your device. If you have questions about your rights under the privacy law where you live, contact us at the address below.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy (for example, if a future version adds a feature that sends data off your device), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the new effective date will be shown at the top. The App Store privacy label will be updated to match.

11. Contact

Overload is published by JPSH GROUP LLC. Questions about this policy or your data: info@jpshgroup.com.
